SAFE Agreement

Simple Agreement for Future Equity

THIS SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity, the "Safe") is entered into as of [DATE], by and between:

Company: Supplyico Limited, a company incorporated in Ireland with registered address at [ADDRESS] (the "Company")

Investor: [INVESTOR NAME], with address at [INVESTOR ADDRESS] (the "Investor")

1. Investment Amount

The Investor hereby invests €[AMOUNT] (the "Purchase Amount") in the Company in exchange for this Safe.

2. Valuation Cap

The "Valuation Cap" is €[CAP — e.g. €2,000,000].

The Valuation Cap represents the maximum pre-money valuation at which this Safe will convert to equity. If the Company raises a priced equity round at a valuation above the Cap, the Investor converts at the Cap, receiving more shares per euro invested.

3. Discount Rate

The "Discount Rate" is [e.g. 20%].

If the Company raises a priced equity round, the Investor may convert at the lower of: (a) the Valuation Cap, or (b) the price per share in the round multiplied by (1 minus the Discount Rate).

4. Conversion Events

4.1 Equity Financing

Upon the closing of an equity financing round (the "Equity Financing") in which the Company issues and sells preferred shares for aggregate gross proceeds of at least €[e.g. 500,000] (excluding conversion of SAFEs), this Safe will automatically convert into shares of the series sold in the Equity Financing at the Conversion Price.

4.2 Liquidity Event

If there is a Liquidity Event (acquisition, IPO, or dissolution) before this Safe converts, the Investor will receive, at the Investor's option, either: (a) a cash payment equal to the Purchase Amount, or (b) conversion into ordinary shares at the Valuation Cap.

4.3 Dissolution Event

If there is a Dissolution Event, the Company will pay the Investor an amount equal to the Purchase Amount, prior to any distribution to ordinary shareholders.

5. Conversion Price

The "Conversion Price" means the lower of:

  1. The Valuation Cap divided by the Company Capitalisation (fully diluted); or
  2. The price per share of the Equity Financing multiplied by (1 minus the Discount Rate).

6. Pro Rata Rights

The Investor shall have the right to participate in subsequent equity financing rounds on a pro rata basis, calculated based on the Investor's ownership percentage at the time of conversion.

7. Most Favoured Nation

If the Company issues any subsequent SAFEs with terms more favourable than this Safe (lower valuation cap or higher discount), the Investor may elect to adopt those more favourable terms.

8. Representations

8.1 Company Representations

The Company represents that: (a) it is duly incorporated and in good standing under Irish law; (b) it has the authority to enter into this Safe; (c) this Safe does not conflict with any existing agreements; (d) the Company's capitalisation table is accurate as disclosed to the Investor.

8.2 Investor Representations

The Investor represents that: (a) they have the authority and capacity to enter into this Safe; (b) they are an experienced investor or have received independent advice; (c) they understand this is a high-risk investment with no guarantee of return.

9. General Terms

  1. Information Rights: The Company will provide the Investor with annual financial statements and quarterly updates until conversion or termination.
  2. No Board Seat: This Safe does not entitle the Investor to a seat on the Board of Directors.
  3. Governing Law: This Safe is governed by the laws of Ireland.
  4. Amendments: This Safe may only be amended with the written consent of both the Company and the Investor.
  5. No Transfer: This Safe is not transferable without the written consent of the Company.
