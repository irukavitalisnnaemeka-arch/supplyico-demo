Terms of Service

Supplyico Limited · Last updated: October 2026

1. Services

Supplyico provides AI-assisted sourcing services including supplier identification, quote benchmarking, negotiation support, quality control coordination, and logistics management ("Services"). All Services are subject to client approval at each decision point.

2. Free Audit

The free sourcing audit benchmarks up to 5 products at no cost. The audit report is the client's property. No obligation to proceed with paid services.

3. Managed Sourcing Fees

Managed sourcing is charged at 25% of verified savings. "Verified savings" means the documented difference between the client's existing supplier pricing and the pricing achieved by Supplyico, confirmed by both parties before invoicing. If no savings are achieved, no fee is due.

4. Quote Check Fees

Quote checking is charged at €49 per quote after the first 3 complimentary quotes. Payment is due upon delivery of the benchmark report.

5. Payment Terms

Invoices are due within 30 days of issue. All amounts are in euros and exclusive of VAT where applicable. Late payments accrue interest at 8% per annum above the ECB base rate, in accordance with the European Late Payment Directive.

6. Client Obligations

The client agrees to: (a) provide accurate product specifications and current pricing; (b) respond to approval requests within 5 business days; (c) not engage directly with suppliers introduced by Supplyico for 12 months after termination without written consent.

7. Supplier Payments

Supplyico facilitates supplier payments but does not hold client funds. All supplier payments are made directly from the client's account or via an agreed escrow arrangement. Supplyico is not liable for supplier non-performance.

8. Quality Control

Pre-shipment QC inspections are conducted by Supplyico or authorised third-party inspectors. QC reports are advisory. The client retains final approval authority. Supplyico is not liable for defects not detectable during standard visual and functional inspection.

9. Intellectual Property

Supplyico's proprietary pricing database, benchmarking algorithms, and AI models remain the exclusive property of Supplyico. Client data (product specifications, order history, supplier communications) remains the client's property.

10. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential all non-public information shared during the engagement. This includes pricing data, supplier identities, margins, and business terms. This obligation survives termination for 2 years.

11. Data Protection

Supplyico processes personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Client data is stored in EU-based infrastructure. A Data Processing Agreement is available upon request.

12. Limitation of Liability

Supplyico's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the client in the 12 months preceding the claim. Supplyico is not liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages including lost profits, lost savings, or supply chain disruption.

13. Termination

Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. Upon termination: (a) outstanding invoices remain due; (b) active orders continue to completion unless mutually agreed otherwise; (c) the non-solicitation clause in Section 6 continues for 12 months.
