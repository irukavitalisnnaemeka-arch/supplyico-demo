3. How we use supplier quotes

Supplier quotes are processed by our AI to extract pricing data. Anonymised pricing data points are added to our benchmarking database. No client-identifying information is included in benchmark data. This aggregated data helps all clients get better pricing.

4. Who we share with

  • AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI) — quote text for analysis, processed under their data processing agreements
  • Cloud hosting (EU-based) — encrypted data storage
  • Payment processor — billing data only
  • QC inspectors — product specs and factory details for inspections you authorise

We never sell personal data. We never share your supplier pricing with competitors.

5. Data storage

Data is stored in EU-based infrastructure. We use encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Backups are encrypted and retained for 30 days.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Restrict — limit how we process your data

To exercise any right, email iruka@supplyico.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Retention

Account data: retained while your account is active + 2 years. Anonymised benchmark data: retained indefinitely (no personal data). Financial records: 6 years (Irish tax law).

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies only (session, authentication). No advertising or tracking cookies. No third-party trackers.

9. Changes

We'll notify you of material changes by email at least 14 days before they take effect.

10. Complaints

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
